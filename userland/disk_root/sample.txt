Ext2 Filesystem Layout in BangOS:
----------------------------------
1. Superblock: Located at offset 1024 bytes (Magic: 0xEF53).
2. Block Group Descriptors: Describes block bitmaps, inode bitmaps, and inode tables.
3. Inode Table: Inode 2 is the root directory ('/').
4. Direct Blocks: i_block[0..11] (up to 12 KB).
5. Indirect Blocks: i_block[12] (single indirect), i_block[13] (double indirect).
